The code

Five percent off your activity booking.

How to use it

Open GetYourGuide with the link above, choose your activity and your date, then go to the order summary.

The promo code field is just before payment. Paste the code, confirm, and check that the discount appears on the total before you pay.

If the field does not appear, it usually means the activity you chose is not eligible. Another activity in the same city often will be.

What it is, and when it is worth it

GetYourGuide sells guided tours, activities and skip the line tickets all over the world. It is the handy reflex when you want to book from your phone, the night before, without going through twenty different websites.

It is especially practical in two cases: timed entry tickets, where booking online saves you a real queue, and guided activities, diving, guided hikes, boat trips, where comparing reviews is worth it.

On the other hand, on the spot and out of season, a local operator often charges less. The platform saves you time and gives you peace of mind, not always money.

Why this code exists

The link on this page is an affiliate link. If you book after clicking, the platform pays me a commission. The price you pay does not change, and it even drops by five percent with the code.

That is what funds the time spent writing the guides on this site. I only list services I use or would recommend without a commission.

Frequently asked questions

Does the code work on every activity?

It applies to eligible activities at the time of booking. The exact amount shows in the summary, before payment.

Does the code cost me anything?

No. The price you pay is the same as or lower than the price shown without the code.

And if it does not work?

Check that it is entered without spaces and that the activity is eligible. Codes have a limited validity, this one may have expired since this page was last updated.
